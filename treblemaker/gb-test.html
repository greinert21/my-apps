Things to try

  1. Connect, then throw your first dart and say what you hit. Does row 1 match?
  2. Try the LED buttons now, while it's connected.
  3. Throw real darts (not finger presses). Any dup rows?
  4. A dart into the black surround. Does it say MISS?
  5. A dart that misses the board completely (wall/floor). Anything?
  6. A bounce-out. Does it register, and as what?
  7. Reset counts, then pile all 9 into T20. Does it say 9 darts?
  8. Walk away 5 minutes. If it drops, does it reconnect by itself when you come back and throw?
